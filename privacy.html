Privacy Policy · Mobile ZG

English · 中文

Effective date: 2026-09-04

Mobile ZG ("we", "our", the "App") is a diary application that showcases stories of Chinese smartphones and new-energy vehicles. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

English

Data We Collect

  • User Content — Diaries, text, images, audio/video you create or upload, and comments. Stored securely in our database (Supabase) to provide the app's core functionality (recording and displaying diaries).
  • Device Identifier — A generated device ID used to distinguish devices and for basic visit statistics.
  • Usage Data — Basic usage statistics (e.g., app opens) to understand how the app is used.
  • Help/Feedback — Messages you submit via the in-app help form (name, email, message) are stored so we can respond.

How We Use Data

  • To provide and improve the diary experience.
  • To show published diaries, comments, and media.
  • To respond to help requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising. Data is stored on our servers (Supabase) and access is restricted.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Contact
